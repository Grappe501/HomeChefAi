Recipe library data

The 284,000+ recipe corpus is structured template data generated and curated by SousChef — not scraped copyrighted recipe text. Matching and browsing use deterministic graph logic. Full recipe display via Clara uses zero credits when sourced from the library.

Agent Suite v6 Phase 4

Complex Clara requests may use pre-built embedding shards over our 284,000+ recipe index (query-only embed at runtime), stream expert synthesis tokens during multi-advisor merges, and log anonymized agent telemetry (tools used, steps, latency) to improve your Brain dashboard. Shard search and streaming synthesis respect the same credit tiers as Phase 3.

Training Kitchen & knowledge graph

53+ technique micro-lessons, 18 taste profiles, 48 cultural cuisine nodes, three Kitchen Academy featured pathways (Executive Chef, Master Baker, Game Show Kitchen), and food source tips are served from our knowledge graph. Browsing and deterministic coaching use no OpenAI credits.

Pantry photos & vision data

Pantry photo scans are processed to extract item names and quantities. Images are sent to our AI vision provider (OpenAI) for parsing. We do not use pantry photos for advertising or sell image data. You review all items before they enter your inventory. Items normalize to sensible kitchen units before storage. See Privacy Policy.

When credits are exhausted

AI generation pauses. You see a clear message with your reset date and upgrade option. No automatic overage billing. Manual entry, recipe library, Kitchen Academy, and deterministic features always available.

Rate limits (abuse protection)

Independent of credits: receipt scan 10/hour, pantry photo 5/hour, assistant 30/hour, meal plan 5/hour per user. Violations may result in temporary suspension per Terms.

Accuracy & abuse

AI outputs may be wrong. Verify allergens, portions, and food safety. Nutrition estimates are approximate. Prohibited: automation bots, scraping, credential sharing across households, prompt flooding, circumventing limits.
