Pantry photos & vision data

Pantry photo scans are processed to extract item names and quantities. Images are sent to our AI vision provider (OpenAI) for parsing. We do not use pantry photos for advertising or sell image data. You review all items before they enter your inventory. See Privacy Policy.

When credits are exhausted

AI generation pauses. You see a clear message with your reset date and upgrade option. No automatic overage billing. Manual entry and deterministic features always available.

Rate limits (abuse protection)

Independent of credits: receipt scan 10/hour, pantry photo 5/hour, assistant 30/hour, meal plan 5/hour per user. Violations may result in temporary suspension per Terms.

Accuracy & abuse

AI outputs may be wrong. Verify allergens, portions, and food safety. Nutrition estimates are approximate. Prohibited: automation bots, scraping, credential sharing across households, prompt flooding, circumventing limits.
