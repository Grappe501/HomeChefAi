What never pauses

  • Manual pantry edits and pantry wizard
  • Viewing Brain insights already generated
  • Deterministic Brain sync (0 credits)
  • Cook logging and inventory subtraction
  • Household sharing and viewing existing meal plans

When credits are exhausted

AI generation pauses. You see a clear message with your reset date and upgrade option. No automatic overage billing. Manual entry always available.

Rate limits (abuse protection)

Independent of credits: receipt scan 10/hour, assistant 30/hour, meal plan 5/hour per user. Violations may result in temporary suspension per Terms.

Accuracy & abuse

AI outputs may be wrong. Verify allergens, portions, and food safety. Nutrition estimates are approximate. Prohibited: automation bots, scraping, credential sharing across households, prompt flooding, circumventing limits.
