Privacy Policy

SousChef learns your kitchen — not to sell you, but to serve you. We build a memory of how your household shops, cooks, and eats so you can waste less and preserve what matters.

Operated by HomeChef AI. Consumer product name: SousChef.

What we do not do

  • Sell personal data or shopping habits
  • Sell food preferences to advertisers
  • Train public AI models on your data (OpenAI API: no training on API data)
  • Share household data across unrelated households

Processors

We use trusted sub-processors to operate the service. See our Sub-processors list for the current vendor table.

Household data

Invited household members can see shared inventory, Brain insights, and cook logs. Each member has their own account. Household admin can remove members. Leaving a household removes access to shared kitchen data; personal account data is retained.

Service is not directed at children under 13. Parental consent required for minors.

AI & receipt processing

  • Receipt images may be sent to OpenAI for OCR — you may use manual entry instead
  • Assistant prompts include pantry/profile context appropriate to your tier
  • Brain 1.0A insights are computed deterministically on our servers — no OpenAI for core math
  • Marketing site Ask mode uses site content only — not your personal pantry
