Privacy Policy

SousChef learns your kitchen — not to sell you, but to serve you. We build a memory of how your household shops, cooks, and eats so you can waste less and preserve what matters.

What we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy
AccountEmail, password hashLogin, recovery
ProfileDietary prefs, allergies, household size, zipPersonalize Sous Chef
InventoryPantry items, quantities, locationsCore product
ReceiptsStore, items, pricesPantry automation
Cook logMeals cooked, ingredients usedBrain + inventory
Brain memoriesPatterns, insightsKitchen Memory
AI interactionsAssistant messages, meal plansFeature delivery
BillingStripe customer ID, tierSubscriptions

What we do not do

  • Sell personal data or shopping habits
  • Sell food preferences to advertisers
  • Train public AI models on your data (OpenAI API: no training on API data)
  • Share household data across unrelated households

Processors

Supabase (database & auth), OpenAI (AI features), Stripe (billing), Netlify (hosting). Sub-processor list maintained here as we add vendors.

Household data

Invited household members can see shared inventory, Brain insights, and cook logs. Each member has their own account. Leaving a household removes access to shared kitchen data.

Your rights

Access, export, and delete your data via Settings or by contacting support. California and EU rights honored as applicable at launch.
