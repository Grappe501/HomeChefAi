Community & Cookbook Rules

These rules apply to neighbor swap, shared recipes, and future cookbook social features. Incorporated by reference in our Terms of Service.

1. Your recipes, your credit

You own original recipes you create. When you publish or share, you grant SousChef a license to display and host them. Always attribute forks to the original author when the app supports lineage.

2. Be honest

Do not misrepresent recipe authorship. Do not post content you do not have rights to share (copyrighted photos, scraped content without permission).

3. Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech, spam, or impersonation. Neighbor swap listings must describe real food items — not unrelated goods or services.

4. Safety

Community features are not food safety inspections. Meet swap partners safely. Verify allergens and freshness yourself.

5. Moderation

We may remove content or suspend accounts that violate these rules. Repeat violations may result in termination per Terms.

6. Status

Neighbor swap is in beta. Full cookbook social launches with explicit notice. Rules may update with 30 days notice for material changes.
